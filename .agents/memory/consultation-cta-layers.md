description: Reference-driven material separation for the shared consultation CTA.

Keep the CTA's pale upper panel distinct from limestone. Reveal a clearly visible, continuous stone inlay and right-side cap between that panel and the flowing teal water; do not let the stone and water collapse into hairline bands. Keep the limestone subtly light rather than dark gray, the white water seam very thin, and caustic shimmer inside the water shape.

**Why:** The reference depends on three visibly separate materials. The earlier mask left the stone and water almost invisible at rendered size, while a full-face texture or overly dark stone obscures that structure.

**How to apply:** Keep the SVG waterline aligned to the CSS water mask and preserve separate masks for the pale panel, exposed stone, and water when changing CTA size, motion, placement, or textures.

The desktop consultation CTA should stay a compact, centered pill with its lettering slightly inset to the right. Keep the same proportions as it narrows on smaller screens.

**Why:** The user corrected the enlarged banner as the wrong size and preferred the earlier compact CTA.

**How to apply:** Keep the desktop dock near its earlier 19.8rem width; shift the copy right modestly without enlarging the badge or the CTA.

On mobile, show the compact fixed consultation CTA only on the homepage, at bottom-right. The menu's own CTA remains available while the navigation is open; respect device safe areas.

**Why:** The user simplified the mobile placement to one persistent CTA on the homepage rather than a dock across every page.

**How to apply:** Reuse the shared CTA surface and arrow, position the mobile dock at the homepage's safe-area-aware bottom-right, and leave other pages to their in-flow CTAs. Keep the separate desktop CTA unchanged unless asked otherwise.

Keep the water caustics irregular and organic rather than a fine uniform grid, with a clear turquoise base and restrained sparkle points.

**Why:** A regular wire-mesh pattern and dark water made the CTA look unlike the bright, natural water in the supplied reference.

**How to apply:** Scale the caustic motif to the CTA instead of repeating it as a tiny tile, and keep highlights limited.

The hover reveal should read immediately as a classic rubber duck: yellow body, orange bill, visible eye, and a small wing detail. Avoid monochrome abstract silhouettes.

**Why:** At CTA size, the previous white outline icon did not clearly read as a duck.

**How to apply:** Keep the colorful silhouette simple enough to remain recognizable at the arrow badge's small size, without changing the existing delayed-hover behavior.
