name: Consultation CTA layers
description: Reference-driven material separation for the shared consultation CTA.
---

Keep the CTA's pale upper panel distinct from limestone. Reveal stone as a shaped inlay between that panel and the flowing teal water, with the stone cap remaining visible at the right. Keep the limestone subtly light rather than dark gray, and keep caustic shimmer inside the water shape; do not stretch stone texture across the full top face.

**Why:** The reference depends on three visibly separate materials; a full-face stone texture obscures that structure, and the earlier dark stone treatment read too heavy.

**How to apply:** Preserve separate masks for the pale panel, exposed stone, and water when changing CTA size, motion, placement, or textures.
