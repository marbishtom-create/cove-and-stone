---
name: Ponte Vedra media standard
description: The content rule for Ponte Vedra portfolio imagery and before/after claims.
---

Ponte Vedra portfolio imagery should use private, enclosed Florida residential settings without Jacksonville skyline or riverfront cues. Until real same-property before-and-after photography is supplied and approved, show the work as a concept direction only and keep the comparison closed.

**Why:** The previous before image and completed image came from different settings, which made the project look misleading and geographically wrong.

**How to apply:** Do not restore completed status, completed photography labels, walkthrough claims, or a before/after slider for Ponte Vedra unless the supplied images clearly match the same property.
